Gather for Slack

Find a time that works without the poll. People reply in their own words, in a thread or a DM, and Gather turns it into a plan your group can agree on.

Your Slack admin may need to approve the install.

1Start with /gatherOr DM Gather one sentence: "plan a happy hour next week in #social".
2Everyone just replies"Thu after 7, vegetarian." Gather asks a follow-up if something's unclear.
3Get a planThe best times, who can come, and suggestions that respect allergies and budgets.
4Close the gapsGather privately asks the people who can't make it whether they could adjust.

What Gather can see

  • Replies in threads under events Gather posted, messages that mention @Gather, and direct messages people send it.
  • While an event is open, a plain message in its channel that sounds like an answer ("Friday works") gets a private "count this?" prompt shown only to its author. Nothing is saved unless they say yes; other conversation is ignored.
  • To remind people who haven't replied, Gather looks up the channel's member list when the organizer asks it to.
  • Each workspace's events and members' preferences are stored separately. Other workspaces never see them.
  • Replies are read by an AI model (OpenAI) to understand availability and preferences.
  • To remove Gather, uninstall it from your workspace's app settings. It stops working right away.
